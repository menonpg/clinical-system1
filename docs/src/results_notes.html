Zero-shot, Kev-4B is the strongest decision model and Laya is near chance. Two cheap local steps change that: an 85-minute fine-tune of Laya on synthetic notes, and a hook that states the smoking arithmetic. Together they give the fastest system in the study, and on hand-written notes the second most accurate. A threshold measured on synthetic notes badly overstates what is safe on real text.

Reading the table. Laya zero-shot misjudges even the plain age question (44% right), which matches its authors' warning that the English noul head can follow its label text. Kev-4B is solid on age (98%) but weak on pack-years (77%) and recency (68%). After fine-tuning on template family A, Laya is near perfect on the unseen family-B templates. The two families share phrase vocabulary, so the hand-written notes below are the honest test.

The arithmetic criteria (pack-years and years since quitting) are where every zero-shot model fails. That is what the computed-facts hook targets.

AAA needs no arithmetic: sex, age and ever-smoked are stated directly. Kev-4B and Gemma 4 solve it zero-shot, and the checklist-AND lifts Kev-0.8B from 0.74 to 0.93 AUROC.

The honest test. On the hand-written notes, fine-tuned Laya keeps AUROC 0.90, with every criterion right on 85% of notes (10% before fine-tuning). With the facts hook it reaches AUROC 0.96 and auto-decides 85% of notes at ≤5% error. Gemma 4 plus facts is the most accurate here (0.99) but about 10× slower. For zero-shot Kev the direct question beats the product (0.87 vs 0.73), because one criterion (recency) drags the product down. Choose which score to threshold on your own labelled notes.

Telling eligible from excluded patients for a trial they are topically matched to is hard. Kev-4B (0.82–0.84) matches the 8B generative baseline (0.80) at roughly half the latency, but at most 7% of pairs can be auto-decided at ≤5% error. Use it to triage what a coordinator reads first, not to decide.

At 124 ms per note, one laptop screens about 29,000 notes an hour, and no note leaves the machine. Ollama's prompt caching keeps Gemma 4 within about 10×.

  • Write one yes/no question per criterion, with thresholds, inclusivity and units spelled out.
  • Start with Kev-4B for zero-shot screening. For the best speed and accuracy, fine-tune Laya on synthetic notes from a seed generator (85 minutes on an M1 Max).
  • State the arithmetic with a deterministic hook. It was the single largest gain on hand-written notes.
  • Keep the per-criterion audit, and choose between the product and the direct question on your labelled set.
