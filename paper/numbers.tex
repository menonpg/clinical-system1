A deterministic hook that states pack-years and years since quitting raised hand-written-note AUROC to 0.997--1.000 for the strongest models; with it, Kev-27B could auto-decide 95\% of hand-written notes at $\leq$5\% error. AAA screening, whose criteria are stated explicitly, was essentially solved by every decision model from 4B upward. On trial prescreening, decision models of 4B--27B reached AUROC 0.82--0.90, above an 8B generative baseline (0.80); only Kev-27B could auto-decide a substantial share of pairs (32\% at $\leq$5\% error).
